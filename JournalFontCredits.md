# Travel Journal font

Travel Journal uses **Xiaolai Regular / 小赖字体**, version **3.126**, for handwritten Chinese and English text.

- Project: https://github.com/lxgw/kose-font
- Original font: https://github.com/lxgw/kose-font/releases/download/v3.126/Xiaolai-Regular.ttf
- Copyright: 2020–2024 LXGW; 2014 Nozomi Seto.
- License: SIL Open Font License 1.1. The unmodified font and full copyright/license notice are bundled in `iOS/Roam/Fonts/`.

The app registers `Xiaolai-Regular.ttf` through `UIAppFonts` and uses its PostScript name, `Xiaolai`. SHA-256: `e2f68daf0e72777a8cf58bc83de1b98634b251e537ddbfca24b0ae50d1802da2`.

Keep the font and `Xiaolai-OFL.txt` together when distributing builds.
